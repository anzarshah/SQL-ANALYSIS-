# 🎵 Music Store Data Analysis Project

## 📚 **Project Overview**  
This project focuses on analyzing data from a music store's database to extract valuable business insights. Using **SQL queries**, we explored various aspects of the music store, such as employee details, customer purchasing behavior, popular music genres, and top-performing artists.

The analysis aims to support decision-making for promotional activities and business strategy.

---

## 🔍 **Key Insights**

1. **Senior Most Employee**  
   The senior-most employee in the music store is **Mohan Madan**, holding the position of **Senior General Manager**.

2. **Employee Hierarchy**  
   The number of employees decreases as the seniority level increases, with more employees at the junior level.

3. **Countries with the Most Invoices**  
   - Most invoices are generated from countries in **Europe**.  
   - However, the **USA (North America)** generates the highest number of invoices overall.

4. **Top 3 Invoice Totals by Country**  
   The top three total invoice values span across **four countries**, showcasing key regions contributing to the store’s revenue.

5. **Cities with Top Customers**  
   The cities with the highest total invoices include:  
   - **Prague** stands out as a top choice for hosting a promotional music festival, as it houses two of the top customers.

6. **Top 5 Customers by Total Spending**  
   The top five customers in terms of the total money spent provide valuable insights for targeting high-value clients.

7. **Most Popular Music Genre by Country**  
   - **Rock music** is the most popular genre in nearly all countries except **Argentina**.

8. **Top 10 Artists with the Most Rock Music**  
   The artist **U2** stands out as the top contributor to the rock music genre.

9. **Top Customers by Artist Spend**  
   The analysis highlights customers who spend the most on specific artists, providing insight into customer preferences.

10. **Top Customers by Overall Music Spend**  
    The top five customers in terms of overall music spending are identified, helping to target marketing campaigns.

---

## 📈 **Conclusions**

- The store employs more people at the **junior level**, while the **senior level** positions are fewer.  
- The majority of customers come from **European countries** (~70%).  
- **Prague** is a key city for revenue, with the highest total invoice amount and two top customers.  
- **Rock music** is the dominant genre across most countries, making it a significant focus for marketing efforts.  
- The artist **U2** is the most prolific in producing rock music.

---

## 🛠 **Technologies Used**  
- **SQL** for data extraction and analysis  
- **Microsoft SQL Server** or similar relational database management systems

---

## 📢 **Recommendations**  
Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended:  
- **Target high-value customers** with personalized promotions and exclusive offers.  
- **Host a promotional event in Prague** to engage top customers and boost brand loyalty.  
- **Focus marketing campaigns on the Rock genre**, as it is the most popular music genre across countries.
