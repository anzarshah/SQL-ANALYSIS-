🎶 Music Store Data Analysis Project
This project focuses on analyzing data from a Music Store to derive valuable business insights. The analysis includes identifying key customer demographics, top-performing music genres, and customer spending patterns, which can be used to improve business strategies and marketing efforts.

📊 Project Overview
The project involves querying a relational database of a music store using SQL and analyzing the results to extract actionable insights. The findings are visualized and summarized in a presentation to highlight key business metrics.

Key Insights:
The senior-most employee is Mohan Madan (Senior General Manager).
USA has the most invoices, while Europe has the highest number of customers.
Prague is the top city by total invoice value.
Rock music is the most popular genre globally (except in Argentina).
The top customer spends significantly on U2, the most prolific rock artist.
💻 Technologies Used
SQL: To query and analyze the relational database.
RDBMS: For managing the music store database.
PowerPoint: For presenting insights and visualizations.
GitHub: For version control and project sharing.

📈 Business Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts on Prague and the USA due to their high total invoice values.
Organize promotional music festivals in Prague, where top customers are located.
Promote Rock music globally, but tailor campaigns for Argentina.
Target top-spending customers with exclusive offers and personalized recommendations.
